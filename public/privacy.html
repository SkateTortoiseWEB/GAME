Privacy

Pawmpeii does not ask for your name or email and has no accounts.

The game sets one cookie, li_id, containing a random ID. It lets the server remember which questions you have played today, your scores and your streak. It lasts one year. If you clear your cookies, that history starts again.

Your browser's local storage remembers that you have seen the welcome screen and your sound setting. It never leaves your device.

The answers you type are stored with your random ID, and are sent to an AI service to check whether they are valid. No other personal data is collected, and nothing is sold or used for advertising.

Questions about this? Contact the site owner.
